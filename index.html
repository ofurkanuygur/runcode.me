What EkstraX reads for you

  • Your statement PDFUpload the PDF your bank sends you. EkstraX extracts every transaction, fee and total — starting with Enpara, more banks in progress.
  • Where your money goesEach transaction is sorted into a category, so spending patterns show up without a spreadsheet.
  • InstallmentsSee every installment plan, how much is left, and how many months it will keep showing up.
  • Month over monthCompare statements to see which categories are growing and which you've brought under control.
  • Next month's balanceA projection of what you'll owe next month, based on installments and what you pay now.
  • The real costInterest, KKDF and BSMV calculated on your actual balance, so minimum vs. full payment is a clear number, not a guess.

Your statement stays yours

A credit card statement is one of the most personal documents you own. EkstraX is designed privacy-first: personal details are masked before any analysis, and your statements are not stored on our servers.

The math — interest, taxes, installments — is done deterministically. The AI layer, powered by Claude, reads the statement and explains the results in plain language.
